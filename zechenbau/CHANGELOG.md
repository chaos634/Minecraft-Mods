# Changelog

All notable changes to Zechenbau are listed here.

## 0.2.0

### Industriekultur

- Seilscheibe (sheave wheel), Hunt (coal tub) and Kauenhaken (pithead bath hanger), each facing the way you place it.
- Cracked and mossy Zechenziegel, for old pits that nature takes back.
- Fix: double slabs drop two slabs and blocks blown up by explosions don't always drop (loot tables in the 26.3 format).

## 0.1.0

- First release: Zechenziegel with stairs, slab and wall, Stahlfachwerk, Fachwerkfenster, Stahlträger, Fördergerüst, Grubenlampe and the Schlägel-und-Eisen tile.
- Recipes, stonecutting, recipe book unlocks, and seven languages.
