# Changelog

All notable changes to Taubenschlag are listed here.

## 0.1.0

- First release: homing pigeons in four plumages, living wild on plains and meadows, tamed with seeds and ringed.
- The Taubenschlag (pigeon loft): tame pigeons settle in, stay around it and fly home to it. Its nest boxes take the post they bring; comparators read how full they are.
- Auflassen: let a pigeon go and it flies home at about 18 blocks per second, getting faster with training, carrying post if you like. Flights go on while nobody is near.
- The Reisekorb (travel basket) to carry pigeons around, let them out, settle them into a new loft, or throw them up in the air with post.
- Young birds from two tame pigeons, six advancements, sounds of their own, and seven languages.
